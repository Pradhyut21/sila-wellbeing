Noticing a change in wellbeing is only the first step.
What happens next?
SILA helps adults in Abu Dhabi move from an early signal
to a visible, human-led care handoff.
This is a working prototype, using synthetic journeys only.
Start with clear choices.
Check-ins are voluntary.
SILA does not listen, watch, or monitor in the background.
Choose the information you want to use, and revoke future sharing
whenever you wish.
Identifiable wellbeing information is never offered to employers.
The five-item WHO wellbeing check-in supports reflection over the past two
weeks.
In this example, a lower wellbeing result prompts a choice to
seek support.
It is not a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, or a prediction
of suicide risk.
Explore support from self-help to primary care, tele-mental health, and specialist
services.
Filter by language and how you prefer to connect.
Listings show official sources and remind you to confirm eligibility, cost,
and availability directly.
No appointment or live service connection is promised.
Before a handoff, review the exact information and recipient.
Share a wellbeing summary, access preferences, or relevant physical health context
separately.
The optional care summary helps a professional consider mental and physical
needs together.
Consent remains specific and visible.
Now the navigator receives a simulated referral.
A human can accept it, decline it with a reason, or
suggest a better route.
The receipt shows what happened and what comes next.
Here, a decline leads to an alternative, with fresh permission before
sharing again.
Need help now is always visible.
Someone reporting immediate danger sees emergency guidance straight away, without waiting
for a score or an AI conversation.
SILA does not claim to monitor emergencies or provide a live
crisis response.
Licensed professionals remain the final decision-makers.
The dashboard makes the connection gap measurable: acknowledgement, contact, waiting time,
follow-up, safety events, and fairness.
Every result shown here is simulated, not a clinical outcome.
SILA makes the next step visible, while the person stays in
control and humans lead care.
