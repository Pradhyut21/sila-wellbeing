## Video direction
Palette and typography come from frame.md. Product is shown through real application screenshots framed at readable scale, with editorial captions above and to the left. Reserved bottom 17% for spoken subtitle strip. Calm staggered reveals paced to the spoken cues; motion uses explicit fromTo entrances and sequential disclosure. Scenes 3 and 7 have deliberate held reads. No bouncing, screensaver drift, front-loaded feature dumps, particle decoration, unverified claims, or illustrative patient images. Clean cuts connect scenes.
